Welcome to Stockly.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the Stockly application.

Information We Collect

Stockly may collect certain information necessary to provide its services and manage user accounts, including:

  • Name or username.
  • Email address.
  • Login information required to access the account.
  • Information and data entered by the user within the application.

We do not request personal information that is unnecessary for operating the application.

Inventory and Product Data

Stockly processes and stores information provided by users to manage their inventory and business operations. This information may include:

  • Product names.
  • Barcodes.
  • Available quantities.
  • Prices.
  • Categories.
  • Companies or brands associated with products.
  • Production and expiration dates, when provided.
  • Warehouse information.
  • Sales and expense information.
  • Customer and debt information, when provided by the user.

This information is used only to provide inventory management, sales management, and related application services.

Camera and Barcode Scanning

Stockly may request access to your device camera when you use the barcode scanning feature.

The camera is used only to scan barcodes and add or search for products. The application does not record photos or videos or collect camera content without the user's knowledge.

Photos and Files

If you choose to add a product image or upload a file, the application accesses only the photos or files that you select for the purpose of providing the requested functionality.

Data Sharing

We do not sell user data.

We do not share personal information or inventory data with third parties except when necessary to operate technical services connected to the application or when required by applicable law.

Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to help protect user data against unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure.

Some information may be stored locally on the user's device. Account and inventory data may also be stored on secure servers when online accounts or synchronization features are used.

Accounts

If the application provides account registration and login, account information may be used to verify the user's identity and provide access to their data.

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their login credentials.

Usage and Technical Data

We may collect limited technical information necessary to improve application performance and diagnose technical issues, such as:

  • Application version.
  • Operating system version.
  • General device information.
  • Crash and technical error information.

This information is not used to directly identify users unless required by a service used by the application.

Children

Stockly is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated data through the options available in the application or by contacting us.

We may retain certain information for a limited period when necessary for legal, security, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time when the application is developed or new features and services are added.

Any updates will be published on this page along with a revised "Last Updated" date.
