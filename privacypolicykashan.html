Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

● Last Updated: November 1, 2026

Welcome to KASHAN. We value your trust and are committed to respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected through our application, how it is used, and how it is protected.

01 Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Name and account details when you register.
  • Phone number and contact information you provide.
  • Delivery address, governorate, and area.
  • Order details, purchased products, and quantities.
  • Product ratings and reviews you submit.
  • Technical information necessary to operate and maintain the application.

You may browse products and place orders as a guest, without creating an account. However, delivery and order-processing information may still be required.

02 How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Receive, process, and prepare customer orders.
  • Deliver products to the address provided.
  • Send order updates and relevant notifications.
  • Respond to customer questions and requests.
  • Improve application performance and user experience.
  • Maintain security and prevent unauthorized activity.

03 Data Storage and Third-Party Services

KASHAN may use Google Firebase services for features such as account authentication, database management, and push notifications.

Information processed through these services may be stored on servers operated by the relevant service providers, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

Learn more: Firebase Privacy and Security

04 Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information may be shared only when necessary:

  • With delivery providers to complete your order.
  • With technical service providers supporting application operations.
  • When required by applicable laws or valid legal requests.

05 Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

06 Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, manage orders, and meet applicable legal or operational requirements.

You may request account deletion and the deletion of associated personal information by contacting us using the email address below.

Certain records may need to be retained where required for legal, security, or accounting purposes.

07 Children's Privacy

KASHAN is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

08 External Links

The application may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

09 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised date. Continued use of the application after changes are published is subject to the updated policy.
