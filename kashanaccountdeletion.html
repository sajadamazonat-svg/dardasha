Account & Data Deletion Request

KASHAN users can request the deletion of their accounts and associated personal data by contacting our support team.

How to Request Account Deletion

1. Click the Request Account Deletion button below.
2. Send your request using the email address registered with your account.
3. Include your registered email address or phone number if required.
4. We will review your request and may contact you to verify account ownership.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Account deletion requests cover the user's account, profile information, contact details, saved addresses, and associated personal data, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Data We May Retain

Certain order and transaction records may be retained when required by applicable laws, accounting obligations, or legitimate legal purposes. Such data will be retained only for the required period.

Need Help?

For questions about account or data deletion, please contact our support team.
