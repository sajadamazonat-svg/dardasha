Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DevNest handles information when you use the DevNest Android application.

Important: DevNest is designed to work locally on your device. The current version does not require an account and does not send your project content to external servers.

1. Information We Collect

DevNest does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

Information that you create inside the application, such as project names, pages, notes, prompts, code snippets, checklists, links, and other project content, is stored locally on your device in the current version.

2. Project Content

DevNest allows you to store developer-related content, including but not limited to:

  • Project names and descriptions
  • Folders and pages
  • Text and notes
  • AI prompts
  • Code snippets
  • Checklists
  • Links
  • Images and files selected by the user
  • Project versions and cloned content

This information is intended to remain on your device unless you explicitly choose to export, copy, share, or otherwise move it outside the application.

3. Clipboard

DevNest provides copy features that allow you to copy text, prompts, code, or other supported content to your device clipboard.

Clipboard data is controlled by the Android operating system and may be accessible to other applications according to your device and Android settings.

4. Images and Files

DevNest may allow you to select images or files from your device for use within your projects.

DevNest does not upload these files to a remote server in the current version.

5. External AI Services

DevNest does not currently provide an integrated artificial intelligence service.

You may choose to copy content from DevNest and manually paste it into an external AI service such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, or another service.

Once you send information to an external service, that information is subject to the privacy policy and terms of that external service. DevNest does not control how third-party AI services process information that you choose to send to them.

6. Export and Sharing

DevNest may provide features that allow you to copy or export project information as text or files.

When you use Android's sharing functionality or another external application to share exported content, the information may be processed by the application or service you choose.

7. Internet Connection

The current version of DevNest is designed to operate primarily offline. The application does not require an internet connection for its core project organization features.

8. Third-Party Services

The current version of DevNest does not use third-party advertising, analytics, authentication, or cloud storage services to collect user project information.

If third-party services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant data collection and processing practices.

9. Data Security

DevNest is designed to keep project information locally on your device. However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for protecting your device and any exported or shared project information.

10. Children's Privacy

DevNest is a general-purpose developer productivity application and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Data Deletion

Because project information is stored locally on your device in the current version, you can delete your projects or application data using the features provided by DevNest or through your Android device settings.

Uninstalling the application may remove locally stored application data, depending on your device and Android backup settings.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to DevNest or its features.

The updated version will include a new "Last updated" date.
