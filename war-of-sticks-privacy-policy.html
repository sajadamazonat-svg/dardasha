This Privacy Policy explains how War of Sticks: Kingdoms (“the Game”) handles information. The Game is currently designed to work offline and does not provide user accounts or sign-in.

1. Information We Collect

In its current version, the Game does not ask you to provide personal information and does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer. The Game does not require registration, login, or an internet connection to play.

2. Local Game Data

Game progress and settings may be stored locally on your device so that gameplay can continue. This information remains on your device and is not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data may delete locally stored progress.

3. Ads, Analytics, and Third Parties

The current version of the Game does not display advertisements and does not use third-party advertising or analytics services to collect information. No personal information is sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties by the Game.

4. Internet and Permissions

The Game is intended to operate offline. It does not need an internet connection for its gameplay features. If a future version adds online features or third-party services, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant data practices.

5. Children's Privacy

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. If you believe that a future version or an integrated service has collected information improperly, please contact the developer using the contact details below.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the current version does not transmit personal information to the developer, the developer does not maintain a personal-information database for the Game. Locally stored game data can generally be removed by clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling it.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the Game's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the same policy URL, with the updated date shown above.
